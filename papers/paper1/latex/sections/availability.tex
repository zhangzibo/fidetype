\section{Code and data availability}
\label{sec:availability}

The specification (\nts{} v0.1), the conformance suite with its golden vectors, the reference implementation (\texttt{fidetype}: adapters, decoders, trust layer, online risk control, provenance ledger) and all experiment scripts and configurations are available under Apache-2.0 at \url{https://github.com/zhangzibo/fidetype}. Every table and figure in this paper is generated from committed result files; the figure script is \texttt{papers/paper1/make\_figures.py}.
