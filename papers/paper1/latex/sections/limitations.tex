\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{One implant participant} in a copy task, with online logits we cannot re-run, is the only non-trivial test of the commit decision; our intervals cover days and deployments of this person, not people. A second public intracortical data set was not analysed.
  \item \textbf{Composed sEMG sentences}: isolated presses with oracle timing, recurring presses, and test sentences from the LM's corpus. 295 of 551 test sentences have a near variant in the LM training text. Rebuilding the LM without them changed trust \ewpm{} by at most $-0.04$, but excluding them from the test set lowered trust \ewpm{} from 8.02, 4.68 and 4.08 to 6.97, 4.08 and 3.69, and made gaze typing faster than the layer on the cross-subject decoder (\cref{app:leakage}). \textbf{MEG is a negative control}: 17 unique test sentences (too few for a sentence-level guarantee) and a decoder that uses key times as training labels.
  \item \textbf{No live users.} Only two user costs are measured, in one gaze-typing user in a non-BCI setting; the miss probability is unmeasured; the MEG and sEMG populations do not match; speed rankings change across plausible variants; the word-completion baseline is a context-free unigram, weaker than clinical AAC. The loss is exact match, which ignores the severity of an error. A user study needs ethics approval.
  \item \textbf{Repeated splits re-use one data set.} The 50 deployments re-draw fit, calibration and test data from the same trials (Brain-to-Text '25) or the same fit sentences (sEMG); their spread is not a CI for new sessions or people. Decoder seeds were repeated only for MEG. Trial order is file order (no time stamps), and the sEMG online order is not a time sequence.
  \item \textbf{Scope of the guarantees.} CRC bounds the mean over deployments, LTT the population risk with probability $1-\delta$, and the online methods a long-run average; all count only observed errors. A guarantee on the error among commits was reachable only for the published decoder. Stream-only gating cannot detect intent mismatch, and the audit log is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof.
\end{enumerate}
