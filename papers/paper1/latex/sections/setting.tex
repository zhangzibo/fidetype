\section{Problem setting}
\label{sec:setting}

\paragraph{Items, streams and actions.} An \emph{item} is one attempt to enter one sentence: a \emph{trial} of the intracortical data, an \emph{instance} (one participant typing one sentence) of the MEG data, or a composed \emph{sentence} of the sEMG data. For item $i$, the decoder emits a stream of $F_i$ frames of log-probabilities $\log p_t(v)$ over $V$ tokens. An LM search returns $K = 10$ ranked candidate sentences with scores, from which we compute $\hat p_{\mathrm{top1}}$, the normalised probability of the top candidate. The system then takes one action: \act{commit} the top candidate (sent without further input); \act{offer} the top $k$ candidates for the user to pick; \act{offer\_words}, the top candidate with uncertain words marked; or \act{abstain}, after which the user types with a fallback keyboard. Let $c_i \in \{0,1\}$ indicate that the top candidate differs from the intended sentence (exact match after normalising case and punctuation), and $f_i \in \{0,1\}$ the wrong-commit loss of the non-commit action ($f_i = 0$ for \act{offer} and \act{abstain}; for \act{offer\_words}, $f_i = 1$ if a wrong word is not marked).

\paragraph{The two error quantities.} Over the $n_{\mathrm{test}}$ test items,
\begin{align}
  \text{wrong-commit rate} &= \#\{\text{wrong commits}\} / n_{\mathrm{test}} \approx \Pr(\text{commit} \wedge \text{wrong}), \\
  \text{error among commits} &= \#\{\text{wrong commits}\} / \#\{\text{commits}\} \approx \Pr(\text{wrong} \mid \text{commit}).
\end{align}
The first is the \emph{marginal} risk: how often wrong sentences reach the recipient per sentence attempted. Our risk controllers bound it, because it is monotone in the commit threshold (\cref{sec:crc}). Committing rarely makes it small, so we always report it with the \emph{commit rate}. The second is the \emph{selective} risk that a user sees in committed text; our controllers do not bound it, and we report it next to every result. \Cref{tab:notation} (\cref{app:notation}) collects the symbols; $\alpha = 0.05$ is the target wrong-commit rate and $\delta = 0.1$ the failure probability of Learn-then-Test.
