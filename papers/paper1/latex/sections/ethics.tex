\section*{Ethics, data and code availability}
\label{sec:ethics}
\label{sec:availability}

This study is a secondary analysis of public, de-identified data sets. No new data were collected and no experiment with human participants was run. The intracortical data come from participant T15 of the BrainGate2 clinical trial (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT00912041), collected under that trial's consent and institutional review board approval \citep{card2024accurate}; the released copy-task file is CC0 \citep{card2025data}. We use only its logits, cue sentences and published sentences, and we did not attempt re-identification. Quoted sentences are public copy-task cues, not the participant's own words. \todo{author decision: final ethics wording; confirm with the JNE editorial office whether this secondary analysis needs any further statement.} No raw recordings or participant data are redistributed (data sets and licences: \cref{tab:licences}).

The \nts{} specification, conformance suite, reference implementation and all experiment scripts are at \url{https://github.com/zhangzibo/fidetype} (Apache-2.0), without weights trained on SpanishBCBL or copied Brain2Qwerty code. Every table and figure is generated from committed result files by committed scripts.
