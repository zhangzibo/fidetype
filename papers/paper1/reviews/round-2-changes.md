## 1. The story changed

The new numbers removed two claims and weakened a third. v2 is rebuilt around what survived.

| v1 claim | New evidence | v2 position |

No value above α is significantly above an exchangeable reference (p ≥ 0.21). Online methods 0.0245–0.0527 (B2T), 0.0381–0.0526 (sEMG) | **No drift claim.** §7.7 now says the earlier excess was a data-reuse artefact plus split variation, and reports this as a methodological lesson (contribution 2, Discussion "Data reuse can fake a drift failure"). ## 3. Guarantee wording (kept precise)
- §3 and §5.4: the controllers bound the **marginal** wrong-commit rate; the error among commits (selective risk) is reported, never claimed as bounded.
- CRC: mean over calibration draws under exchangeability *and* separate calibration data. LTT: probability ≥ 1 − δ over calibration draws for the population risk at the chosen threshold. ACI: long-run average of *observed* losses, any sequence; a-priori bound needs scores ≤ 1 and f ≡ 0; silent errors are outside it (noticed-only runs show it).
- Random-split CRC is called an implementation check, not a test of the assumption.
