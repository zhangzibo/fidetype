# Neural-data law table

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** This page is design information for an open-source software project. It is not legal advice. It is not complete. Laws change often. Read the current official text, and ask a lawyer before any pilot, product or data collection. The legal review is roadmap item P5-06 (externally blocked: it needs a lawyer; user-owned).

**Status:** checked by web research on 2026-11-17 (roadmap P5-04). Each row has a confidence mark:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P** | Verified from a primary source (legislature, official gazette, govinfo, UNESCO) that we opened |
| **S** | Verified from a secondary source only (law-firm brief, FPF, bill tracker). The official page did not load for us |
| **U** | Uncertain. Sources disagree, or we could not check it |

Some official sites did not load for our fetch tool (leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, cga.ct.gov, edps.europa.eu, eur-lex, congress.gov, bills.legmt.gov). Rows that depend on them are **S** or **U**.

## 2. What the definitions differ on

| Instrument | Nervous system | Data inferred from non-neural signals |
|---|---|---|
| Colorado | CNS + PNS | No explicit exclusion; identification purpose |
| California SB 1223, AB 1883 | CNS + PNS | Excluded |
| Montana | CNS + PNS | Excluded ("nonneural information") |
| Connecticut, Vermont Act 145, Delaware | CNS only | Not stated |
| MIND Act (bill) | CNS + PNS | A separate category ("other related data") |
| UNESCO | CNS + PNS | Included |

**Why this matters for Fidetype:** sEMG (KT-sEMG, emg2qwerty, Toronto sEMG) measures muscle activity driven by the **peripheral** nervous system. Under the CNS + PNS definitions it can be neural data. Under the CNS-only definitions it may not be. Montana's exclusion of "motor activity" may also exclude it. MEG and intracortical data are CNS data under every definition.

## 3. What the software does about it

> **Our take:** The common duties across these instruments are: treat neural data as sensitive, get opt-in consent for named purposes, keep less data, let people delete their data, and keep a record. The tools below support these duties. They do not make a deployment compliant.

| Duty (common pattern) | Fidetype tool | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Consent for named purposes, withdrawable | Opaque `consent.ref` (SEC-6); `ConsentRecord` on the operator side with purposes, expiry, withdrawal; `check_consent_ref` | `fidetype.trust.privacy`; `UserProfile.consent_ref` |
| No personal data in streams and logs | Collector `privacy` processor: keep / drop / hash per `meta` key, PII scan, text drop or HMAC | [collector docs](../../docs/collector.md) |
| Data minimisation | Default privacy policy drops `subject.*`, `session.*`, `user.*` and unknown keys; NTS has no raw samples (SEC-1) | `DEFAULT_POLICY` |
| Deletion on request | Delete the profile; redact the subject's decisions in the ledger (tombstones keep the chain verifiable); a deletion receipt | `privacy.erase`, `ProvenanceLedger.redact_subject` |
| Record of processing | Hash-chained provenance ledger with lineage and licence flags | [provenance ledger](../concepts/provenance-ledger.md) |
| No workplace emotion inference (EU AI Act Art. 5(1)(f); California AB 1883 for neural data at work) | Not a feature of Fidetype. Do not build it | — |

> **Our take:** The strictest common rule is the safest default: treat sEMG as neural data, ask for opt-in consent per purpose, and support deletion. Our own data collection is externally blocked until ethics approval and the legal review (P5-06).

> **Contradiction:** the [landscape source](../sources/neural-input-landscape-2026.md) gives California SB 1223 as "CCPA regulations from 1 Jan 2026". Our sources give the statute's effective date as 1 Jan 2025. The 2026 date may refer to later CCPA regulations, not to the statute. We keep both claims until the official text is read (P5-06). The landscape also marked Delaware HB 380 "UNVERIFIED"; it is now verified from a secondary source (signed 2 Sep 2026, effective 1 Jan 2027).
