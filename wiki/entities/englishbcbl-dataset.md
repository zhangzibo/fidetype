# EnglishBCBL

- **Status**: **embargoed until journal publication** of v2. The study class `PinetAudio2025` is also absent from the released code.
- **Contents**:
  - Recordings: 9 participants × 10 sessions × about 1 h of MEG (about 90 h), plus an anatomical MRI for each participant.
  - Stimuli: 2,724 unique English sentences generated by Llama 4, each heard as audio and then typed **once** per participant. 22,000 typed sentences in total.
- **Why it matters**: it has 10× more data per subject than [SpanishBCBL](spanishbcbl-dataset.md) and far more sentence diversity. It is what makes [asynchronous decoding](../concepts/synchronous-vs-asynchronous-decoding.md) competitive ([v2](../sources/brain2qwerty-v2.md)).

> **To watch:** check whether it gets released once v2 is published in a journal.
