# BCBL (San Sebastián, Spain)

https://www.bcbl.eu/en

BCBL is the research centre where both Brain2Qwerty datasets were recorded. It has a MEGIN 306-channel MEG and a 3T Siemens Prisma MRI. BCBL **owns the data**. The v1 data are shared on request or through gated HuggingFace access, and the v2 data are embargoed.

- [SpanishBCBL](spanishbcbl-dataset.md) (v1)
- [EnglishBCBL](englishbcbl-dataset.md) (v2)
