# Meta FAIR Brain & AI (Paris)

The Brain & AI team at Meta AI (FAIR) in Paris. It is led by **Jean-Rémi King**, with core members including **Stéphane d'Ascoli**, **Jarod Lévy**, **Jérémy Rapin** and **Hubert Banville**. Mingfang (Lucy) Zhang (ENS/PSL) is co-first author on v2.

## Output relevant to us
- [Brain2Qwerty v1](../sources/brain2qwerty-v1.md) and [v2](../sources/brain2qwerty-v2.md), with [code](../sources/brain2qwerty-code.md).
- Défossez et al. 2023: decoding speech perception from MEG. This is the origin of the [BrainModule](../concepts/brainmodule.md). *Not yet ingested.*
- d'Ascoli et al. 2025: decoding individual words from non-invasive recordings (Nat. Commun.). *Not yet ingested.*
- Zhang et al. 2025: the companion neuroscience paper on typing (arXiv 2502.07429). *Not yet ingested.*
- Libraries: `neuralset` (King et al. 2026, arXiv 2605.03169), `neuraltrain`, `exca`.
