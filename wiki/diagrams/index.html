1. What Brain2Qwerty does

Meta's model reads brain signals while a person types. Then it predicts the typed text. The signal comes from finger movement, not from thought.

Source: 01-what-brain2qwerty-does.drawio · Wiki: sources/brain2qwerty-v1.md

2. Version 1 and version 2

v1 needs the time of each key press. v2 does not. v2 uses 10 times more data per person and an LLM to write the sentence.

Source: 02-v1-vs-v2-pipeline.drawio · Wiki: sources/brain2qwerty-v2.md

5. Proposed roadmap

Start with learning. Then build an open toolkit. Then do research on a small real-time decoder.

Source: 05-roadmap.drawio · Wiki: questions/research-directions.md

6. Architecture: six layers

Six layers turn neural data into reliable text. The trust core is the heart of the project. Green parts are open source; red dashed parts stay private later.

Source: 06-architecture-layers.drawio · Wiki: questions/architecture.md

7. Data flow

Data goes from a source through a decoder into the NeuralTextStream format. The trust core then picks an action: commit, offer choices, ask, or abstain.

Source: 07-data-flow.drawio · Wiki: questions/architecture.md

8. Roadmap (trust-layer direction)

Set up first. Then write two papers: calibrated text, then causal decoding. Then release the benchmark and connect real devices.

Source: 08-roadmap-v2.drawio · Wiki: questions/direction-decision.md

9. Milestones (1-week target)

The user set the targets on 2026-11-08. M0 ends on 11-09. M1 and M2 run in parallel, then M3 and M4, then M5, then the code parts of M6 and M7. All end by 11-15. The dashed lane holds items that depend on outside people. They have no dates.

Source: 09-milestones.drawio · Wiki: questions/project-plan.md

10. Open-source path

Six stages take Fidetype from a paper to a neutral standard, then to a company. Each stage borrows from SGLang, LangChain, OpenTelemetry or BIDS. Success means decoders that emit NeuralTextStream, not stars.

Source: 10-open-source-path.drawio · Wiki: questions/open-source-playbook.md

11. Long-term roadmap: from v0.1 to a full standard

After v0.1, eleven phases run in four lanes. We build the spec, SDKs, collector, adapters, trust layer, benchmark and demo. Grey dashed items depend on outside people. Replay comes before hardware, and the demo and benchmark come before outreach.

Source: 11-roadmap-standard.drawio · Wiki: questions/roadmap-standard.md
