Safe WPM v0.2 is the headline column since 2026-11-22 (ADOPTED on that date): prefix credit and a reading cost of 0.5 s + 0.3 s per committed word for every commit (user model aac-gaze-v1). For an entry that commits whole sentences, v0.2 is the v0.1 value with the reading time added. The v0.1 column keeps every earlier number with its value and its v0.1 label. Entries on tracks v0.1 got their v0.2 value on 2026-11-24 from the same per-trial decisions as their v0.1 value. Definition: docs/safe-wpm.md section 6a. In the entry history, each Safe WPM value names its version.

Pool scope: the Guarantee column is a bound on the wrong-commit rate of the whole scored test set of an entry, with all its users, participants, sessions, days or runs together. It is not a bound for each user, session or day: one of them can be worse than the pool. A mark in square brackets in the Guarantee column (for example [SAFETY FINDING: ...] or [LABEL-BLIND COMMIT STEP: ...]) points to a recorded safety finding; the track page gives the details and the numbers. Details: docs/trust-guarantee.md section 4.

2026-11-25: LABEL-BLIND COMMIT STEP: no evidence of signal. Only 14 of the 437 calibration (val) trials can commit at all: the other rules of the trust policy block the rest. 13 of these 14 are wrong (the top-1 sentence is right for 1 of 437 val trials). Because 14 / 437 is below alpha 0.05, every threshold meets the LTT bound, so LTT takes the lowest one and commits all 14, whatever the labels are. With permuted labels it commits the same 14 items. On test, 14 of 15 commits are wrong. The bound holds (test wrong-commit rate 0.022), but only because the policy almost never commits: the commit step shows no evidence that the score separates right from wrong text. Read this entry as a vacuous guard result, not as evidence that the guard works on this decoder. Basis: the pooled-guard check (commit 74a21f8; https://github.com/zhangzibo/fidetype/blob/main/experiments/2026-11-25-pooled-guard-check/results.md). No number changed.
