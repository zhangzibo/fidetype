## Headline answers
1. **"Drift" does not survive the clean protocol.** With CRC calibration data disjoint from the temperature / conformal / policy-grid data, offline CRC gave 0.0245 and 0.0600 (B2T ours, two splits), 0.0478 / 0.0490 (published gate), 0.0563 / 0.0399 / 0 (sEMG cross-session / calib / cross-subject). No value is significantly above exchangeable random splits (p ≥ 0.21). The old 0.0625 / 0.0635 used the fit data for calibration. A small calibration-vs-test level shift exists on B2T ours in one split (p = 0.012), but there CRC stayed below α. Online methods stay at 0.025–0.053.
2. **The fair comparator is always-offer.** Trust beats never-commit by +0.18 eWPM (B2T ours) and +0.66 (published), and by about 0 elsewhere (on sEMG cross-session / cross-subject and MEG, trust *is* always-offer).
3. **Error among commits** under trust: 0.25 (B2T ours), 0.09 (published); under clean CRC 0.61 (sEMG cross-session) and 0.95 (MEG).
4. **The guard hurts on B2T** (no guard: 0.036 vs 0.059; 0.025 vs 0.044). **The frame temperature degrades decoding** on weak decoders: sEMG cross-subject trust 4.08 → 4.75 (decode raw) → **4.84** (LM weight re-tuned after calibration), above gaze 4.27.
5. **LM-assisted gaze typing** (k = 3 word completion) gives 4.10 on MEG and 6.19 on sEMG: above trust on MEG (3.20) and sEMG cross-session / cross-subject. B2T and sEMG session-calibrated stay above it.
6. **Leakage is real but harmless:** 310 / 1,717 B2T cues and 295 / 551 sEMG test sentences have an exact or near variant in the LM training text; removing them leaves the main numbers unchanged (B2T identical; sEMG ≤ −0.04 eWPM).
