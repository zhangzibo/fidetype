## Headline answers
1. **Forward-chaining (time order):** calibrating on earlier days and testing on the next day, LTT max kept the pooled wrong-commit rate at 0.010–0.027 in all variants. It exceeded α on 0–2 of 10–13 test days, never significantly (CP lower bound > α on 0 days). Plug-in failed on up to 6 of 10 days.
2. **Per-day risk is not controlled:** published gate, day 34, LTT max = 0.102 over the 50 splits (0.098 over 200; > α in 94 % of splits); fixed splits 8/50 = 0.16 [0.07, 0.29]. Our decoder ≤ 0.049 on every day. LTT bounds the day mixture, not each day.
3. **Population risk:** App. R's 0.5 % / 1.5 % is biased low; the exact (with-replacement) population exceedance of LTT is 4.7 % / 4.1 % (≤ δ). Test-set exceedance of LTT max: 4/50 = 0.08 [0.02, 0.19], 1/50 = 0.02 [0.001, 0.11]; with 200 splits 0.075 / 0.055. LTT max vs CRC max: McNemar p = 0.25 / 0.031 (50 splits), 5e-7 / 1e-7 (200 splits).
4. **Joint LTT (valid guarantee for both targets)** with β = 0.10 commits nothing on our decoder, sEMG, MEG and both published fixed splits; 7 % on the published gate over splits (EAC 0.065, wrong-commit 0.005). With β = 0.20: published 32 %, EAC 0.06.
5. **No-guard (plain threshold):** on real data it is as safe or safer (plain + LTT max: ours 0/200 splits > α, mean 0.030; published 0.024). **But it committed 203 of 300 uniform (no-information) control streams on our decoder** (68 % wrong) and 2–3 of 300 frame-shuffled streams. The guard is what rejects these. The frozen configuration "plain threshold + LTT max" fails the control test.
6. **Published-gate bonus 2.0** was set by hand in the first commit (3fb8154) and never tuned. Bonus 0.5–4 changes LTT max rows little (0.022–0.039 fixed; 0.028–0.033 over splits); bonus 0 commits less and is 1.3 eWPM slower. The published sentence alone (no confidence from our decoder) never commits: the gate is a two-decoder agreement signal.
7. **Reuse, all 12 comparisons:** after Holm and with an overlap-respecting day jackknife, only B2T ours shows an effect (CRC pure +0.009, jackknife Holm 0.004; CRC max +0.007, Holm 0.046). With 200 splits ours CRC pure is +0.003 (Holm n.s.). sEMG LTT-pure lowering does not survive (jackknife p 0.28).
8. **Clean-up verified:** MEG Tab. 1 CIs [3.01, 3.44] and [3.02, 3.31]; 0/816 one-sided bound 0.0037 (0.0045 is two-sided); sEMG snapping 0.38 %; "same policy without commits" = λ = +∞ with the same fit-A pipeline, gains +0.114 (MC SE 0.005) / +0.510 (0.007); always commit beats trust in 9/9 user models only on the published gate (5/9 ours).
9. **ECE / AUROC with CIs:** B2T ours ECE 0.448 → 0.067, AUROC 0.797 [0.773, 0.819]; published 0.102 → 0.058, AUROC 0.710 [0.678, 0.744]; sEMG 0.83 / 0.76 / 0.67; reliability data + PNG.
