On 2026-11-13 the user said the review and publishing process makes papers take about 50-60x longer than planned. Decouple paper writing from the research project.

**Why:** peer review, endorsement and venue steps run on outside timelines. They must not block or set the pace of the research and engineering work.

**How to apply:**
- Keep paper tasks (drafting, preprints, venue choice, submission, endorsement, peer review, revisions) in a separate publications track. That track has no deadline and does not count toward M1–M7 or P-milestone progress.
- Research tasks (experiments, decoders, trust layer, spec, SDKs) never wait for a paper step. Results go into the wiki/experiments first. Papers draw on them later.
- Do not plan research work around paper deadlines. Do not report a milestone as late because a paper step is pending.
